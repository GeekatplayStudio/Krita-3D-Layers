Geekatplay 3D Layers

Turn a layer into a 3D model, pose and light it, and place it in your image as a layer you can change any time.

Start

  1. Restart Krita after installing or enabling the plugin.
  2. Open an image and choose Settings › Dockers › 3D Layers.
  3. No 3D service yet? On the Create tab click Try the sample model. The 3D editor opens in its own window, over your image. Pose and light the model, then click Place in Document.
  4. To make your own models, add a key for Meshy, Tripo or Hitem3D, or the address of your ComfyUI, on the docker's Settings tab. Then select a layer (a cut-out object on a transparent background works best) and click Generate 3D model. When the job says Ready, click Pose & place.
  5. To change a 3D layer later, select it and click Edit pose & light (or Tools › Scripts › Edit 3D Layer).

The 3D editor runs in a Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome window, because Krita plugins cannot show 3D themselves. It talks only to Krita, on this computer.
